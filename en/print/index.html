What you can print

  • Sudoku — A workout for the mind, in numbers
  • Word Search — Words hidden in the letter grid
  • Mental Math — Add and take away to wake the mind
  • Word Scramble — Put the jumbled letters back in order
  • Colour by Number — Fill in the colours, number by number
  • Spot the Difference — An eye for what has changed
  • Maze — Trace the way out with your eyes
  • Math Crossword — Sums that add up across and down
  • Copy the Pattern — An eye for copying what you see
  • Dot to Dot — Join the dots and a picture appears

How to print

  1. Open the home screen and tap “Print puzzles” near the bottom.
  2. Choose which game, which level and how many sheets.
  3. Tap “Make it” and the print window opens.
  4. To save a file instead of printing, set the printer to “Microsoft Print to PDF” or “Save as PDF”.

Times and scores are never printed on a worksheet. Answer sheets are optional.

I went looking online for puzzles for my mother, who lives with dementia, and found it far too hard to find good ones — so I made these for her. I am putting them here in the hope that they help someone else too.
