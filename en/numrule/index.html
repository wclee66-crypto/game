Number Pattern — Fill in the missing number. It has five levels, from a gentle first step to a real challenge. Print the worksheet on A4 paper for free, or download it as a PDF.

Five levels

  • 1. First steps — 1 to 10 · counting up by 1s or 2s
  • 2. Gentle — 1 to 20 · counting up a little at a time
  • 3. Easy — Skip counting by 2s, 3s, 5s or 10s
  • 4. Normal — Counting up or down
  • 5. Hard — Counting up or down with bigger numbers

Scoring

LevelThe row gets longer and the numbers get bigger — Level 1: by 1s or 2s · Level 2: by 1s to 3s · Level 3: by 2, 3, 5 or 10 · Level 4: counting up or down · Level 5: bigger numbers, up or down
How to playFind the pattern as the numbers steadily go up or down, then type the missing number on the number pad
How to answerNo confirm button — the moment you tap an answer, it is scored
Correct answersUp to 600 · in proportion to correct answers
Time bonusUp to 300 · only if you finish, in proportion to time left
Streak bonusUp to 100 · 3 in a row 25 / 4 in a row 50 / 5 in a row 75 / 6 or more 100
Wrong-answer penaltyNone — a wrong answer just shows the right one and moves on
Level bonusNormal +100, Hard +250 (when you finish)
Top scoreLevels 1–3: 1,000 · Normal: 1,100 · Hard: 1,250
KeyboardOn a PC, type with the number keys and erase with Backspace. Answers are checked automatically
