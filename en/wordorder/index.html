Saerok is a brain-training playground where games that help with early dementia and mild cognitive decline are free to play. It works on a computer, a tablet or a phone, and if the screen is hard to use you can print the puzzles instead.

Download the worksheets (PDF)

Download and print straight away — no sign-up, no email. Each file holds four worksheets and the answer sheets.

These files are prepared in advance, so they always hold the same puzzles. For a fresh set every time, use “Print puzzles” on the site itself.

Five levels

  • 1. First steps — Three-letter words
  • 2. Gentle — Three and four-letter words
  • 3. Easy — Four and five-letter words
  • 4. Normal — Five and six-letter words
  • 5. Hard — Six letters and longer

Scoring

How to playLook at the jumbled word and tap the letters below in order. Tap a filled blank again to take that letter back
LevelLevel 1 three letters · Level 2 three to four · Level 3 four to five · Level 4 five to six · Level 5 six and longer
Letters to choose fromThe word’s own letters are laid out along with a few that do not belong. Just leave the spare ones alone
HintTap “Show hint” for a one-line clue. It costs you nothing
Correct answersUp to 600 · in proportion to correct answers
Time bonusUp to 300 · only if you finish, in proportion to time left
Streak bonusUp to 100 · 3 in a row 25 / 4 in a row 50 / 5 in a row 75 / 6 or more 100
Wrong-answer penaltyNone — if you slip, the correct word is shown and you move on
Level bonusNormal +100, Hard +250 (when you finish)
Top scoreLevels 1–3: 1,000 · Normal: 1,100 · Hard: 1,250
KeyboardOn a computer: Backspace deletes a letter, Enter checks

I went looking online for puzzles for my mother, who lives with dementia, and found it far too hard to find good ones — so I made these for her. I am putting them here in the hope that they help someone else too.
