Privacy Policy

Saerok is a free brain-training website you can use without signing up. Here is exactly what is stored and where.

No accounts, no sign-in

We never ask for your name, phone number or address.

Your scores stay on your device

Scores and settings (such as text size) are saved only in your own browser and are never sent to a Saerok server. (Saerok has no server.)

Clearing your browser data also clears your scores. You can keep a copy with "Export" in Settings.

Visitor statistics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages they read. It uses cookies and collects anonymous statistics only (pages visited, device type, approximate region). It cannot tell us your name or contact details.

You can opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on (tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

Advertising (Google AdSense)

To keep Saerok free, we may show Google AdSense ads. Ads are never placed on the game screens or on printed worksheets.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com), and opt out of other vendors' personalized ads at www.aboutads.info.

Suggestions

Messages sent with "Suggest" (and your email, if you add one) are delivered to us by the Web3Forms service. We use them only to reply to you.

Contact

Please use "Suggest" at the bottom of the home screen.
